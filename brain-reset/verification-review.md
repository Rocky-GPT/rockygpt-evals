This independent agent semantic review compares realistic student conversations with the trusted records actually published for RockyGPT. The [verified checkpoint](checkpoints/2026-09-04/verified-checkpoint.json), also available as [readable answers](checkpoints/2026-09-04/verified-checkpoint.md), covers 20 conversations and 27 turns. No material unsupported campus claim remains in this reviewed answer set. It is a composite of the last complete run and the successful password-reset rerun after the collection-discovery correction, not a complete fresh run of the final runtime. No human review was performed; raw reports retain `human_review: "pending"` and agent review is recorded separately.

## Method and evidence

The database audit used read-only transactions against active release `v2-20260904145456`, activated September 4, 2026 at 15:01:54 UTC. The retrieval adapter pins one release per turn. I independently loaded the cited structured rows, published artifacts, and exact document chunks from that release, checked source identity and freshness, and compared their contents with the answers. I also checked the shuttle ingestion contract and published timetable to establish which times mean leaving campus, calling at a destination, and returning to campus.

Citation presence alone is not evidence of correctness. This review checks whether the source supports the specific claim, its date and scope, and whether the response handles missing evidence honestly. No credentials or unrelated database snapshots are included in this checkpoint.

## Corrections discovered during evaluation

Early runs exposed substantive errors despite passing basic response/citation checks: missing menu venue caused false unavailability; ambiguous shuttle endpoint fields produced incomplete returns; an event room was promoted into a general location; and omitted date arguments selected Friday when the question meant Saturday. The adapter now derives venue and meal labels from published artifacts, preserves explicit shuttle endpoint semantics, and requires dates for date-dependent collections. General model instructions address search refinement, source scope, and travel-time roles without matching user phrases.

A later complete run caught the model searching only documents and contacts for password reset, overlooking the published critical fact. Collection descriptions and a general check of concise verified facts before declaring information unavailable address this discovery failure. The corrected runtime's targeted password-reset response searched the correct collection and returned the verified reset URL and Help Desk contact. Its next full run completed the library case correctly, then provider credit exhaustion prevented the remaining requests. The provider reported HTTP 429 with `insufficient_quota` / `credit_balance_exhausted`. No additional credits were purchased and no further model calls were made after that diagnosis.

Consequently, the checkpoint preserves the last complete 20-conversation run with its failed password answer replaced by the successful targeted rerun. The failed attempts and revision history are retained separately. The complete final runtime has not been verified across all 27 turns after the collection-description change; those global effects remain a validation limitation even though the corrected case and final-runtime library case passed.

## Limits

This is a sample-based semantic review of 20 conversations and 27 turns, not a proof of correctness for arbitrary questions. Published data lacks library hours, a map/location feed, live course enrollment, and private student records. Menu coverage has date gaps, including September 7–8; an absent menu does not mean a venue is closed. Static schedules do not confirm real-time operations. Unit checks separately exercise stale/unknown evidence, date validation, provenance, read-only SQL, deadline caps, and TLS configuration; the live corpus primarily uses fresh published data. The data adapter's 29 focused regression tests, Ruff, and mypy passed.
