# Area part of each writer's brief

Each writer got [common.md](common.md) plus one of these. Writers did not read any files, run code or
browse the web.

- **people (pp-01..15):** sophomore business, junior biology and first-year students looking for
  people: a professor's email, office or office hours, who teaches a subject, who runs a school or
  office (deans, president, provost, directors), who to contact in an office, a professor's
  background. Real names only when sure; otherwise by role.
- **events_clubs (ec-01..15):** residents and commuters in early November: what's on tonight / this
  week / this weekend, events by theme, whether a kind of club exists, how to join or contact a club,
  when it meets, Greek life, student government meetings.
- **academics (ac-01..15):** students planning next semester: what a course is about, prerequisites
  and credits, major or minor requirements, which school a program is in, whether a program exists,
  gen ed, catalog policies (pass/fail, repeats, withdrawal, probation, dean's list, credit load).
- **howto (ht-01..15):** commuter, resident, transfer and student-athlete getting something done:
  parking, ID cards, housing, meal plans, holds, transcripts, counseling or health, accommodations,
  public safety, printing, wifi or accounts, career help, office hours and locations.
